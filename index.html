Connect to Google Drive & Google Sheets

Configure your Drive folder, target Sheet, or an Apps Script webhook so rows & photo links stay aligned with your master audit sheet.

Quick Setup Options for Live Drive Upload & Sheet Append:

  1. Option A — Google Apps Script Webhook (Easiest on Mobile): Create a bound script on your Google Sheet with doPost(e) that decodes base64 image data into DriveApp.getFolderById(folderId).createFile(...), sets sharing so the photo link is accessible, and calls sheet.appendRow([...]). Deploy as a Web App ("Execute as: Me", "Who has access: Anyone with link" or restricted) and paste the URL above.
  2. Option B — Google Identity Services (GIS) + Drive v3 & Sheets v4 REST API: In Google Cloud Console, enable Google Drive API and Google Sheets API, configure an OAuth 2.0 Web Client ID with your app origin authorized, request scopes drive.file and spreadsheets via google.accounts.oauth2.initTokenClient, upload multipart/related to drive/v3/files, and append via spreadsheets/{id}/values/A:I:append?valueInputOption=USER_ENTERED.
  3. Option C — Zero-Config Crate Workflow (Active by Default): Snap photos on your phone, review the AI appraisal & price range, copy the TSV row directly into Google Sheets (or download CSV), and paste ready-made Discogs/eBay listings whenever you sell.
